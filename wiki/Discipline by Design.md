# Discipline by Design

The source argues that durable discipline is built by reducing dependence on willpower. Because effort is immediate while many rewards are delayed, make the desired behaviour easier, more rewarding now, and more automatic.

## Practical strategies

1. **Temptation bundling** — pair a necessary task with an immediate reward that is otherwise reserved for that task.
2. **Small checkpoints** — break large tasks into visible, completable units to create progress feedback.
3. **Cues and routines** — use a consistent cue to reduce the repeated decision to begin.
4. **Identity-led goals** — frame a behaviour as an expression of the person you are becoming, not only an outcome you want.
5. **Environment design** — remove friction before the task and make distractions less convenient.

## Core idea

Do not rely on repeatedly overriding impulses. Design the conditions in which the desired action becomes the easier default.

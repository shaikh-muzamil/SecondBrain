# Simplicity in Business

The source presents complexity as a cost that makes companies, products, and decision-making harder to operate. The practical aim is to convert complexity into simpler choices, structures, and interfaces.

## Principles

- **Focus the offering** — fewer, clearer products make a business easier for customers and teams to understand.
- **Keep teams small and accountable** — small autonomous teams with one goal reduce communication overhead.
- **Use existing abstractions** — build on reliable tools when they remove complexity, instead of rebuilding everything from scratch.
- **Avoid feature accumulation** — more features can weaken clarity and distract from product-market fit.
- **Assign clear ownership** — one person or team should own a responsibility rather than spreading it across many people.
- **Do work manually before automating it** — understand the process and demand before adding systems around it.

# Hormozi Skills

`hormozi-skills` is an agent-oriented skill library for turning a business idea or existing offer into a structured offer system inspired by Alex Hormozi's frameworks.

## What it produces

The repository describes an end-to-end workflow covering market research, offer design, positioning angles, value perception, bonuses, pricing, objection handling, pitches, hooks, and landing-page copy.

## Workflow

An orchestrator takes an idea, offer, or sales material; asks focused intake questions; identifies the current funnel stage; then delegates work in this order:

1. Market research
2. Offer design
3. Value and pricing work in parallel
4. Sales assets

The individual skills can also be used independently for tasks such as offer audits, pricing strategy, landing-page copy, hooks, or objection handling.

## Underlying framework

The system draws on Grand Slam Offer construction, value perception, obstacle-to-solution mapping, guarantee design, price anchoring, and hook architecture. Its aim is not generic copy generation but a coherent offer system.
